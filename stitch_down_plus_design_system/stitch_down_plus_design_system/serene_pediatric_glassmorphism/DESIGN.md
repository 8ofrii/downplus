## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-calming, deeply supportive sanctuary for caregivers, clinicians, and pediatric patients navigating Down syndrome developmental care. Medical interfaces frequently introduce unnecessary cognitive fatigue through sterile clinical palettes, razor-sharp geometric edges, and alarmist status signifiers. This system actively reverses that tension, prioritizing emotional reassurance, clarity, and gentle optimism.

The design movement synthesizes **Organic Soft Glassmorphism** with **Tactile Biophilic Modernism**. Glass panels do not act as cold, futuristic acrylics; instead, they evoke luminous sea-glass, misty morning air, and soft water droplets. Translucent frosted planes, whisper-soft gradients, and generous pillowy radii construct a physical space that feels safe to touch and effortless to navigate. 

Every interaction must reduce physiological stress. Visual elements float across subtle, organic pastel meshes rather than harsh stark-white plains. Feedback is gentle and non-punitive, ensuring that complex therapy schedules, milestone trackers, and developmental metrics convey calm competence and tender clinical excellence.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in tranquil, restorative tones engineered to avoid sensory overstimulation while maintaining precise clinical clarity:

- **Primary (`#6C9A8B` — Ethereal Sage):** Anchors core actions, high-level headers, and confirmed milestones. It evokes renewal, growth, and gentle medical grounding.
- **Secondary (`#7CA5B8` — Muted Sky Teal):** Serves observational flows, therapy tracking, communication logs, and supporting informative metrics.
- **Tertiary (`#E3A857` — Gentle Champagne Amber):** Provides warm, uplifting moments of encouragement, attention, and achievement without the anxiety traditionally sparked by harsh warning ambers.
- **Neutral (`#606B66` — Soft Mineral Slate):** Provides soft, legible contrast for typography and borders, entirely avoiding high-strain pure blacks (`#000000`).

### Supporting Tint & Canvas Roles
- **Canvas Base:** Soft warm cloud tint (`#F7FAF8` to `#F4F7F9`).
- **Glass Panel Surface:** Translucent cloud white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)` varying from `0.65` to `0.85` based on elevation depth).
- **Subtle Pastel Ambient Mesh:** Soft underlying blooms featuring diluted sage (`#E2EDE8`), dawn lavender-teal (`#E6EEF4`), and pale buttercup amber (`#FDF5E8`).
- **Glass Borders:** Luminous white strokes (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`) coupled with delicate inner glows.

## Typography

The type system is led by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly, rounded terminals, open counters, and high readability under stress. It avoids clinical rigidity while retaining structural balance.

- Headings utilize balanced weights (`600` and `700`) with tight negative letter tracking to build an intimate, human connection.
- Body text retains relaxed, tall line-heights (`1.6` to `1.65`) to optimize scanability for parents and practitioners tracking therapy notes in demanding environments.
- Labels are set with slight tracking to preserve clarity even when placed over frosted, translucent panels.
- For multilingual and Arabic language implementations, pair seamlessly with **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**, maintaining matched scale, weight equivalents, and line-height baselines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a **Breathable Fluid Grid** that avoids cramped dashboard densities:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with generous margins (`2.5rem`) and airy gutters (`1.5rem`). Content boundaries should rest comfortably at a maximum reading container of `1280px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins. Glass panels condense gracefully into single and dual-column arrangements.
- **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout with compact outer margins (`1.25rem`) and gutters (`1rem`). Complex tables reflow into vertical, pillowy stacked cards.

Vertical rhythm is intentionally expansive. Generous spacing tokens (`space-lg` and `space-xl`) between distinct functional domains allow the gentle backdrop gradients to peek through, preventing sensory overload.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is defined through **Luminous Frosted Glassmorphism** rather than opaque dropshadows:

1. **Backdrop Blur Levels:**
   - **Base Glass Panels & Cards:** `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.72)`.
   - **Floating Modals & Dropdowns:** `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)` with surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`.
   - **Subtle Overlays & Inactive Shelves:** `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` with surface `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.55)`.

2. **Borders & Edge Lighting:**
   - Cards and structural panels feature a continuous `1px` translucent border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`.
   - Add a subtle top-lit inner highlight: `inset 0 1px 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`.

3. **Ambient Pillowy Shadows:**
   - Soft, diffused, and cool-tinted. Standard elevation is expressed as: `box-shadow: 0 8px 32px 0 rgba(31, 38, 135, 0.06), 0 2px 8px 0 rgba(108, 154, 139, 0.04)`.
   - Elevated states (modals, active drags) expand to: `box-shadow: 0 16px 48px 0 rgba(31, 38, 135, 0.09), 0 4px 12px 0 rgba(124, 165, 184, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The shape system embraces **Tactile Organic Softness** (`roundedness: 3`). Rigid corners and sharp acute angles are systematically excluded from the interface to convey safety, approachability, and gentleness:

- **Cards & Primary Modules:** Styled with generous `rounded-3xl` (1.75rem to 2rem / 28px–32px), creating smooth, stone-like contours.
- **Buttons & Interactive Tags:** Full pill silhouettes (`border-radius: 9999px`) to invite gentle, confident touch interactions.
- **Form Inputs & Search Fields:** Styled with `rounded-2xl` (1rem to 1.25rem) to balance generous curves with interior text padding.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Sage (`#6C9A8B`) gradient to `#5C897B`, text `#FFFFFF`, rounded pill shape (`9999px`), `shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(108, 154, 139, 0.25)`. On hover: subtle scale `1.02` with amplified glow.
- **Secondary Glass Action:** Frosted white (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.65)`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`, text `#4A5852`, pill-shaped, backdrop-blur `16px`.
- **Tertiary Warm Action:** Soft champagne tint (`rgba(227, 168, 87, 0.15)`), text `#966723`, pill-shaped, no hard border.

### Cards & Care Modules
- Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)`, backdrop blur `18px`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, border-radius `28px`.
- Ambient shadow `0 8px 32px rgba(31, 38, 135, 0.06)`. Internal padding scales across `space-md` to `space-lg`.

### Chips & Milestone Badges
- Continuous pill shape, height `32px`, padding `0 14px`.
- In-progress badges use soft teal (`rgba(124, 165, 184, 0.15)` with text `#4A768A`).
- Completed celebration badges use gentle warm amber (`rgba(227, 168, 87, 0.16)` with text `#8E5F1E`).

### Input Fields
- Fill `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.95)`, border-radius `16px`.
- Focus state: Surface turns `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`, border transitions to `#7CA5B8` with an ethereal ring `0 0 0 4px rgba(124, 165, 184, 0.18)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Generously sized (22px x 22px) with curved edges (checkbox `8px`, radio `50%`).
- Unchecked: Frosted white with `1.5px` border of `rgba(96, 107, 102, 0.25)`.
- Checked: Soft Sage fill (`#6C9A8B`) with crisp white rounded checkmark icon.

### Specialized Pediatric Care Components
- **Milestone Blossom (Progress Arc):** Soft, rounded segmented rings utilizing primary sage and secondary sky teal fills with glowing endpoints.
- **Sensory Mood Logger:** Pillowy glass sliders with tactile circular thumbs and warm, friendly emoji-hued milestone markers.
- **Gentle Notice Banners:** Frosted cards with pastel amber or teal left-side glow bars instead of intrusive error alerts.
